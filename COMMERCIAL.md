# Commercial Licensing & Enterprise FAQ

**Voice Pipes** is source-available under the [Business Source License 1.1 (BSL 1.1)](LICENSE), developed and maintained by **Myned AI Ltd.**.

Our goal is to keep Voice Pipes completely free and open for individual developers, researchers, startups, and small-to-midsize businesses, while ensuring sustainable development when large enterprises or cloud service providers monetize the runtime at scale.

---

## 1. Who Can Use Voice Pipes for Free?

Under the **Additional Use Grant** in our [LICENSE](LICENSE), you can use Voice Pipes in production **at no cost** if **both** of the following are true:

1. **Annual Turnover Under \$5,000,000 USD:** The total aggregate annual revenue of your organization and all of its affiliates (measured over the most recent 12-month fiscal period or preceding calendar year) does not exceed **\$5,000,000 USD**.
2. **No Competing Managed / Hosted Service:** You do not offer Voice Pipes (or substantially similar functionality) directly or indirectly as a hosted, managed, PaaS, or SaaS offering to third parties for a fee or commercial consideration.

In addition, **non-production use** (evaluation, local development, testing, benchmarking, academic research, and personal projects) is **always free for everyone**, regardless of company size.

---

## 2. Who Needs a Commercial License?

You must obtain a Commercial License from **Myned AI Ltd.** if:

- Your organization (including parent companies, subsidiaries, and affiliates) has **more than \$5,000,000 USD** in annual turnover and you wish to run Voice Pipes in production; **OR**
- You wish to embed, host, or resell Voice Pipes as a **managed cloud service, PaaS, or commercial voice API/platform** for third-party customers.

---

## 3. What Does a Commercial License Include?

A commercial agreement with Myned AI Ltd. provides:

- **Unrestricted Production & Revenue Rights:** Removes the \$5M annual turnover ceiling for your organization and its affiliates.
- **Managed Hosting / SaaS Rights (Optional):** Custom licensing tiers for cloud providers, BPOs, and platforms offering hosted voice infrastructure to third parties.
- **No BSL Restrictions:** Perpetual or annual commercial rights independent of the 3-year BSL Change Date window.
- **Enterprise Support & SLAs:** Direct engineering support, custom provider integrations, latency tuning, and indemnification options.

---

## 4. What Happens After the 3-Year Change Date?

Under BSL 1.1, each release of Voice Pipes automatically converts to the **Apache License, Version 2.0** (an OSI-approved Open Source license) **three years** after that specific version is published.
